How these numbers are worked out
  • Read live from your Fitness Coach Tracker Google Sheet (Daily Log, Workout Log, Profile) every time the page opens or you press Refresh.
  • The 7-day window ends on the last complete day before today. Averages skip days where that field wasn't logged.
  • The 7-day average weight is the mean of all weigh-ins in the 7 calendar days up to each date.
  • Targets come from the Profile tab, so change them there.
